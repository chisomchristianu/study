# Circular Economy

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 14, 19–20](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Focus | Scope |
|---|---|
| Circular Economy | Lecture concepts plus labelled explanation |

Design for disassembly makes joints and material combinations separable. Material passports record composition, origin and recycling information. Closed-loop recycling returns material to an equivalent application; the lecture contrasts this with open-loop use in a lower-grade application.

```text
Material -> manufacture -> service -> disassembly -> sorting -> recycling
   ^                                                            |
   +------------------------------------------------------------+
```

Bolted connections may help separation; inseparable mixed-material laminates can obstruct it.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Supplement: open-loop recycling means a different product system and does not always imply downcycling. Actual recyclability also depends on collection, contamination, economics and available infrastructure.
