# Social Impacts and Policy

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 11–12, 38](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Raw material | Lecture concern |
|---|---|
| Cobalt | Unsafe artisanal mining and child labour risks |
| Lithium | Water use and community conflicts in arid regions |
| Bauxite | Red mud and land use |
| Iron ore | Tailings dam failures |
| Rare earths | Chemical and radioactive residues |

The lecture discusses LkSG, CSDDD, the EU Battery Regulation, CBAM and ESPR. It gives EU targets of −55% by 2030 and neutrality by 2050, and German targets of −65% by 2030, −88% by 2040 and neutrality by 2045, relative to 1990 for the percentage reductions.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> These are slide summaries, not verified current legal guidance. Scope, implementation dates and exemptions must be checked against official sources. Risks differ between mines and suppliers.
