# Carbon Footprint and Material Selection

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 13, 19–20](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

Lecture estimates, kg CO₂e per kg material:

| Material route | Approximate footprint |
|---|---|
| Blast-furnace steel | 2 |
| Scrap electric-arc steel | 0.4–0.7 |
| Primary aluminium | 8–16 |
| Secondary aluminium | 0.5–1 |
| Cement | 0.6–0.9 |
| PE or PP | 2–3 |
| Carbon fibres | 20–40 |

For a screening calculation: embodied emissions = mass × emission factor. Example: 10 kg primary aluminium at 12 kg CO₂e/kg gives 120 kg CO₂e; secondary material at 0.8 gives 8 kg CO₂e. Select for strength, cost, emissions, circularity and climate resilience together.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Compare equivalent functions and lifetimes. Electricity mix, allocation, processing, transport and system boundaries affect results; low emissions per kg do not alone identify the best component.
