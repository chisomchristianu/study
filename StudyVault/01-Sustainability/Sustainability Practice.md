## Question 1 [recall]

> What are the two directions connecting materials and climate?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Production causes emissions; climate changes the service requirements.

## Question 2 [recall]

> Why is air temperature insufficient for design?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Sun-exposed components can become much hotter than ambient air.

## Question 3 [recall]

> What is design for disassembly?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Designing joints and material combinations to permit separation at end of life.

## Question 4 [recall]

> What does a material passport record?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Composition, origin and recycling-related information.

## Question 5 [recall]

> Name two social or ecological extraction risks.

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Examples: unsafe cobalt mining; water stress associated with lithium extraction.

## Question 6 [recall]

> Which factors accompany strength and cost in sustainable selection?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Emissions, circularity, resilience and service life, alongside other impact categories.

## Question 7 [application]

> Estimate emissions for 5 kg aluminium at 10 kg CO₂e/kg.

> [!answer]- Show answer
> 5 × 10 = 50 kg CO₂e.

## Question 8 [application]

> A polymer housing sits in a sunlit car. Which conditions must be checked?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Component temperature, duration, loading and grade-specific softening/creep/ageing data.

## Question 9 [analysis]

> Why can a lighter high-carbon material still be preferable?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Use-phase savings may offset production impacts; compare equal function and lifetime with consistent boundaries.

## Question 10 [analysis]

> A recyclable fibre is bonded permanently into a mixed assembly. Is the product circular?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Not necessarily: separation, collection, processing feasibility and retained material value govern the actual loop.

> [!summary]- Pattern summary
> Definitions support calculations; calculations require conditions; selection requires tradeoffs.
