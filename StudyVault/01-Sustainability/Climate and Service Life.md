# Climate and Service Life

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 5–10, 15–20, 69](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Focus | Scope |
|---|---|
| Climate and Service Life | Lecture concepts plus labelled explanation |

Materials production contributes to emissions; a changing climate also changes service requirements. The lecture cites floods, heat damage, low water and hail as examples. Design against component temperature, moisture, loading rate and time throughout service life, rather than air temperature alone.

Air at 35 °C can correspond to rails above 60 °C, asphalt at 50–70 °C and PV modules at 60–80 °C (lecture examples).

```text
Climate exposure -> component conditions -> property changes -> failure risk
       ^                                               |
       +----------- material production emissions -----+
```

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Temperature trends and event statistics are lecture claims, not independently updated measurements. A single year above 1.5 °C is not the same as a sustained breach of the Paris long-term temperature goal.
