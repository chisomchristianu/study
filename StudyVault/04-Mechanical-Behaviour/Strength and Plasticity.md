# Strength and Plasticity

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 59–61](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Focus | Scope |
|---|---|
| Strength and Plasticity | Lecture concepts plus labelled explanation |

Strength is the stress capacity before a defined unacceptable deformation or fracture. Supplement: yield strength marks a specified onset of plastic flow, while ultimate tensile strength is the maximum engineering tensile stress. Plasticity leaves residual strain after unloading and enables forging and rolling.

```text
Load -> elastic response -> yielding -> plastic deformation -> fracture
          unload: return              unload: residual strain
```

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Some materials have no clear yield point; a proof stress such as Rp0.2 may be used. Strength depends on temperature, rate and loading mode. Ultimate engineering stress is not generally the fracture stress.
