# Component Stiffness

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 55](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

Supplement reconstructing the component relationships:

| Measure | Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Axial rigidity | EA | Material modulus × area |
| Axial spring stiffness | EA/L | Force/displacement for a uniform bar |
| Bending rigidity | EI | Modulus × second moment of area |
| Torsional rigidity | GJ | Circular shaft, appropriate torsion constant |
| Torsional spring stiffness | GJ/L | Torque/twist angle |

A uniform axially loaded bar has δ = FL/(EA). Doubling area doubles axial stiffness; doubling length halves it. Bending response also depends on supports and loading.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Rigidity and spring stiffness have different units. For noncircular sections J is a torsion constant, generally not the polar area moment.
