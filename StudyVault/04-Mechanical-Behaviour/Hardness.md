# Hardness

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 58](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Focus | Scope |
|---|---|
| Hardness | Lecture concepts plus labelled explanation |

Hardness is resistance to indentation by a harder test body. Bonding, crystal structure, microstructure, grain size, alloying and heat treatment affect it. Supplement: Brinell, Vickers and Rockwell use different indenters and evaluation methods. Hardness often informs wear and machining decisions.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Hardness is not toughness or a universal wear predictor. Conversions to tensile strength are empirical and material-specific; compare scales and test conditions carefully.
