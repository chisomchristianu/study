# Stress Strain and Thermal Expansion

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 41–45, 43](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

Standard textbook reconstruction of SVG-rendered equations:

| Quantity | Formula | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering strain | ε = (L−L₀)/L₀ | Reference length L₀ |
| Engineering stress | σ = F/A₀ | Original area |
| Thermal strain | εth = αΔT | Constant α approximation |
| Fully restrained thermal stress | σ = −EαΔT | 1D linear elasticity; tension positive |

Steel α≈12×10⁻⁶/K; aluminium ≈23×10⁻⁶/K; concrete ≈10–12×10⁻⁶/K; thermoplastics ≈50–150×10⁻⁶/K (lecture examples). A free 1 m steel bar heated 50 K expands 0.6 mm. If fully restrained with E=200 GPa, ideal compressive stress is 120 MPa.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Free, uniform expansion creates no stress. Partial restraint, thermal gradients, yielding or buckling change the fully restrained estimate. N/mm² = MPa.
