# Material Symmetry

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 46](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

Supplement to the lecture enumeration:

| Symmetry | Meaning | Linear elastic constants |
|---|---|---|
| Isotropy | Same properties in every direction | 2 |
| Transverse isotropy | Isotropic within one plane | 5 |
| Orthotropy | Three orthogonal material symmetry planes | 9 |
| General anisotropy | Direction-dependent without those reductions | Up to 21 |

Rolled sheets, wood and fibre composites may require directional properties.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Counts assume classical 3D linear elasticity with usual energy symmetry. A polycrystal may be approximately isotropic at one scale but textured at another.
