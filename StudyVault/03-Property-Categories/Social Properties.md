# Social Properties

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 38, 11](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Focus | Scope |
|---|---|
| Social Properties | Lecture concepts plus labelled explanation |

Social properties concern origin and extraction conditions, worker safety, human rights and conflict-mineral risks. Supplement: assess supplier traceability, due diligence, grievance processes and credible evidence about working conditions. Material passports may support information exchange but do not themselves prove ethical sourcing.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Country or material labels alone do not establish conditions at a specific operation. Compliance paperwork and actual outcomes should be distinguished.
