# Ecological Properties

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 37, 13–14](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Focus | Scope |
|---|---|
| Ecological Properties | Lecture concepts plus labelled explanation |

Ecological assessment covers production and recycling emissions, resource consumption, circularity and impacts over the life cycle. Supplement: life-cycle assessment defines a functional unit and system boundary, inventories inputs/outputs, and evaluates impact categories. A lighter component can reduce operating energy but require more energy-intensive production.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Carbon footprint is one impact category; water, toxicity, land use and end-of-life losses may change the decision. Recycling credits depend on accounting assumptions.
