# Mechanical Properties Overview

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 32, 39–40](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Property | Question answered |
|---|---|
| Stiffness | How much elastic deformation? |
| Strength | What stress causes unacceptable deformation or failure? |
| Hardness | How resistant to local indentation? |
| Ductility | How much plastic deformation before fracture? |
| Toughness | How much energy absorbed before fracture? |
| Fatigue | What happens under repeated loading? |
| Wear | What material is lost through contact and motion? |

Supplement: fatigue can initiate and propagate cracks below monotonic strength; wear depends on the whole contact system, lubrication and environment.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> No single property ranks a material for every application. Fatigue and wear data require test conditions and geometry.
