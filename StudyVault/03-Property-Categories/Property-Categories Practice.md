## Question 1 [recall]

> What does specific heat capacity measure?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Energy per mass per temperature change.

## Question 2 [recall]

> What does thermal conductivity describe?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Ability to conduct heat.

## Question 3 [recall]

> How are scalar resistivity and conductivity related?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> They are reciprocals in a linear scalar conductor.

## Question 4 [recall]

> What is hardenability?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Ability of steel to harden to a given depth under specified cooling conditions.

## Question 5 [recall]

> What are the three ingredients of stress corrosion cracking?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Susceptible material, a suitable environment and tensile stress.

## Question 6 [recall]

> What is a social property concern?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Working conditions, origin, human rights or conflict-mineral risk.

## Question 7 [application]

> A conductor doubles in length at unchanged area and resistivity. What happens to R?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> R = ρe L/A, so resistance doubles.

## Question 8 [application]

> A part handles acid at elevated temperature. Which category requires priority?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Chemical compatibility/corrosion resistance under the actual acid, temperature and stress conditions.

## Question 9 [analysis]

> Compare hardness and hardenability.

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Hardness concerns indentation resistance; hardenability concerns depth of hardening response.

## Question 10 [analysis]

> Can carbon footprint alone define sustainability?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> No: resource use, water, toxicity, service life, circularity and social effects can alter selection.

> [!summary]- Pattern summary
> Definitions support calculations; calculations require conditions; selection requires tradeoffs.
