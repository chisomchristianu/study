# Technological Properties

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 36](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

Supplement:

| Property | Processing question |
|---|---|
| Formability | Can the shape change without unacceptable cracking? |
| Machinability | Can cutting achieve acceptable tool life and surface quality? |
| Weldability | Can a sound joint be made under specified conditions? |
| Castability | Can a mould fill and solidify into a suitable component? |
| Hardenability | How deeply can a steel harden under a given cooling treatment? |

Composition, microstructure, geometry and process route interact.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Hardenability is not the same as final hardness. These are process-dependent assessments, not universal material constants.
