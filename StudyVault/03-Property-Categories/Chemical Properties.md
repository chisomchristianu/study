# Chemical Properties

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 35, 18](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Focus | Scope |
|---|---|
| Chemical Properties | Lecture concepts plus labelled explanation |

Corrosion resistance, reactivity, resistance to acids/alkalis/solvents and oxidation behaviour characterize environmental compatibility. Supplement: corrosion depends on chemistry, temperature, potential and microstructure. Stress corrosion cracking requires a susceptible material, a suitable environment and tensile stress. The lecture uses ageing bridge prestressing steel as a case study.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Corrosion-resistant does not mean immune. Passivating alloys may suffer localized corrosion. The specific bridge-cause attribution is a lecture statement, not an independently reviewed investigation here.
