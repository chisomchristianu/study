# Thermal Properties

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 33, 16–17, 43](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Property | Meaning | Typical unit |
|---|---|---|
| Thermal conductivity k | Heat conduction ability | W/(m K) |
| Expansion coefficient α | Strain per temperature change | K⁻¹ |
| Specific heat c | Energy per mass and temperature change | J/(kg K) |
| Melting temperature | Solid–liquid transformation | °C or K |
| Glass transition | Change in amorphous-phase mobility | °C or K |

Supplement: heat capacity affects energy storage, while conductivity affects heat flow. Neither directly gives allowable service temperature.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Properties vary with temperature, direction and phase. Celsius temperature differences equal kelvin differences, but absolute-temperature equations require kelvin.
