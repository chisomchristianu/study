# Electrical and Magnetic Properties

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 34](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

Supplement:

| Property | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Conductivity σe | Current-carrying response to electric field |
| Resistivity ρe | Reciprocal of conductivity for a scalar linear conductor |
| Dielectric behaviour | Polarization, insulation and breakdown behaviour |
| Magnetic permeability μ | Magnetic response, B = μH in a linear model |
| Ferromagnetism | Strong response, domains and possible hysteresis |
| Paramagnetism | Weak positive magnetic susceptibility |

For a uniform conductor R = ρe L/A.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Conductivity and permeability may be tensors, nonlinear or frequency-dependent. Supplement: diamagnetism is a weak negative response, completing the basic comparison.
