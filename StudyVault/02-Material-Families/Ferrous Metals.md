# Ferrous Metals

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 21–24](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Focus | Scope |
|---|---|
| Ferrous Metals | Lecture concepts plus labelled explanation |

Steel and cast iron are iron-based material families. Supplement: steels typically contain less than roughly 2.1 wt% carbon; cast irons usually contain more, with the precise classification depending on alloy system and standard. Steel can combine useful strength, toughness and formability. Cast irons offer casting advantages and composition-dependent wear and damping properties.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Cast iron is not uniformly brittle: graphite morphology matters, especially the distinction between flake and nodular graphite. Heat treatment and microstructure strongly affect both families.
