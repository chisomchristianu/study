# Fibre Composites

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 29, 43](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Focus | Scope |
|---|---|
| Fibre Composites | Lecture concepts plus labelled explanation |

Supplement: fibres provide direction-dependent reinforcement while the matrix transfers load, protects fibres and maintains shape. CFRP uses carbon fibres in a polymer matrix. Fibre orientation, volume fraction, interface quality and lay-up control properties. The lecture gives near-zero thermal expansion along the fibre direction as an approximate example.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Properties transverse to fibres can differ greatly. Delamination, matrix softening, impact damage and recycling difficulty may limit use. Near-zero axial expansion is lay-up dependent.
