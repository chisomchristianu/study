# Polymers

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 22, 16, 54, 57](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

Supplement to the brief family overview:

| Class | Structure and response |
|---|---|
| Thermoplastic | Usually softens on heating and can be reshaped |
| Thermoset | Crosslinked network; cannot simply be remelted |
| Elastomer | Flexible network with large recoverable deformation |

Polymers are sensitive to temperature, loading rate and time. The lecture gives E ≈2–5 GPa for some thermoplastics/thermosets and highlights creep and softening.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Glass transition concerns amorphous phases and is not melting. Grade, reinforcement, moisture and temperature alter behaviour; not every polymer fits the indicative ranges.
