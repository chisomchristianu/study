# Glasses

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 28, 68](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Focus | Scope |
|---|---|
| Glasses | Lecture concepts plus labelled explanation |

Supplement: glasses are amorphous solids lacking long-range crystalline order. Silicate glass is familiar, but not all glasses have the same chemistry. Many are transparent, chemically durable and brittle. Glass transition describes a change in relaxation behaviour rather than a sharp crystalline melting event. The lecture lists glass toughness around 0.01 MJ/m³ as an illustrative value.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> Surface flaws dominate practical tensile strength. Tempering can introduce beneficial surface compression; transparency is not universal.
