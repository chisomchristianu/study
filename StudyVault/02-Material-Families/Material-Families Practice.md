## Question 1 [recall]

> What distinguishes ferrous and non-ferrous metals?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Ferrous materials are primarily iron-based; non-ferrous families are not.

## Question 2 [recall]

> Which metal does the lecture associate with good conductivity?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Copper.

## Question 3 [recall]

> Which metal is highlighted for lightweight construction?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Magnesium.

## Question 4 [recall]

> What distinguishes a glass structure?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> It lacks long-range crystalline order.

## Question 5 [recall]

> What roles do fibres and matrix serve?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Fibres reinforce; the matrix transfers load, protects and holds fibres.

## Question 6 [recall]

> Name the three basic polymer classes.

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Thermoplastics, thermosets and elastomers.

## Question 7 [application]

> Select a candidate family for an electrically insulating wear-resistant hot component.

> [!answer]- Show answer
> A suitable engineering ceramic is a candidate; verify toughness, thermal shock and actual electrical behaviour.

## Question 8 [application]

> Which property direction matters for a unidirectional CFRP tie?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> The fibre direction and loading alignment; verify transverse/interface constraints too.

## Question 9 [analysis]

> Why is “all cast iron is brittle” unreliable?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Graphite morphology and microstructure matter; nodular graphite cast iron can have useful ductility.

## Question 10 [analysis]

> Why is material-family selection insufficient for a real component?

> [!answer]- Show answer
> Alloy/grade, processing, microstructure, geometry, defects and service conditions determine actual performance.

> [!summary]- Pattern summary
> Definitions support calculations; calculations require conditions; selection requires tradeoffs.
