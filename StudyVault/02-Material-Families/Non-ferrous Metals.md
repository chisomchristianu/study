# Non-ferrous Metals

#materials-science #concept

Source: [lecture, slides 25–26, 54](https://cwillberg.github.io/Lectures/stream_material_science/stream_wst_01/). Explanatory additions are identified as supplements.

| Metal | Main lecture association |
|---|---|
| Copper | High electrical and thermal conductivity |
| Magnesium | Lightweight construction |
| Titanium | Strength, heat resistance and corrosion resistance |
| Nickel | Corrosion and high-temperature resistance |
| Aluminium | Supplement: low density and widely used lightweight alloys |
| Tungsten | Supplement: high modulus; lecture table lists E ≈360 GPa |

Non-ferrous means the material is not primarily iron-based. Select a specific alloy and condition rather than a family name alone.

> [!warning] Conditions and limits
> High conductivity is not the same as high strength. Heat resistance is relative to alloy, environment and duty; magnesium corrosion and fire considerations depend on form and application.

| Trigger | Reasoning |

| Typical distinction | Electrical conductor: copper; very low-density metal: magnesium. |
